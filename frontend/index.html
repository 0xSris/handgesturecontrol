How it works

From camera frame to computer action

1Webcam captures frame
2MediaPipe detects hand landmarks
3Gesture rules classify pose and pinch distance
4Smoothing and cooldown remove accidental triggers
5Action engine controls mouse, keyboard, volume, browser, or sharing

Applied AI answer

Yes, this fits Applied AI, but be honest about training.

Did I train the model? No. The project uses a pretrained MediaPipe hand landmark model.

Where is the AI? The AI model detects hand keypoints in real time from camera images.

What did I build? The practical AI application around it: gesture classification, smoothing, action mapping, safety lock, calibration, file sharing, and packaging.

How to phrase it: I integrated a pretrained computer vision model and engineered the decision layer that turns predictions into real computer actions.

Opening

Show tracking

Show controls

I unlock with an open palm, move the cursor by pointing, pinch to click, and switch modes with three fingers.

Show transfer

In share mode, the app starts a local transfer server and shows a QR code. The phone scans it and downloads the file.

Explain safety

I added smoothing, cooldowns, preview mode, and a hold-to-lock gesture so random hand motion does not trigger actions.
